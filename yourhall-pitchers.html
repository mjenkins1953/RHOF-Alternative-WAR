Every pitcher in the 1,000+ career-IP pool (majors plus the Negro Major Leagues), re-scored in your browser: five per-season z-scores against that season's league mean and spread among 50+ IP pitchers — ERA and WHIP (both inverted, so a lower-than-league mark is positive), strikeout rate, winning percentage against .500, and saves — blended by your weights, each season scaled to 200 IP and summed across the pitcher's 40+ IP seasons.
