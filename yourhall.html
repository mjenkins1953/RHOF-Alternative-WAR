Every player is still scored against his own seasons — league and position, no cross-era skew, below-average years still dampened, same as the main list — but with your emphasis on contact, power, defense, patience and speed. The sliders start on the standard SAA formula. The Hall size slider sets how many players make your hall — cut it to the top 100, or open it up to 500.

On the bubble

The twenty next in line under your weights — nudge a slider and the line moves.

Every hitter in the 5,000+ career-PA pool (plus Negro Leagues players clearing 9 season-equivalents), re-scored in your browser: five per-season z-scores against that season's mean and spread among 200+ PA hitters — batting average, isolated power, walk rate, stolen-base rate, defensive value, the bar for the first four set 60% of the way toward the player's own position — blended by your weights, each season scaled to 600 PA (below-average seasons at 40% weight in the career total), then combined as your Career ↔ Peak setting dictates (the SAA default is a 50/50 average of the career total and the best seven seasons). Source: Lahman database hitting/fielding (2025 release, including Negro Leagues statistics); Hall of Fame membership current through the 2026 induction class. Your weights, blend, hall size and search are remembered in this browser so your hall is still here next visit — never sent anywhere. It's your hall.
